# Covenant Army Rules (11e homebrew)

_Source: Codex: Covenant v5.5.0 in 11th-edition wording. Caste Hierarchy is the revised version, and Regenerating Shields is rolled for each unit._

## Caste Hierarchy

**Sergeants:** A Sergeant model's entry lists the Caste Doctrines it can issue.

**Auxiliaries:** A unit can include Auxiliary models at the rate printed on its datasheet (for example, for every 5 Unggoy Warriors, 1 Sangheili Auxiliary or up to 2 Yig-Yar Auxiliaries). There is one Auxiliary entry per species: Sangheili, Jiralhanae, Yig-Yar, T'vaoan, Unggoy, Huragok and Yanme'e. T'vaoan Auxiliaries have the YIG-YAR keyword, so they can be taken wherever Yig-Yar Auxiliaries can. Each Auxiliary model can take one Specialist ability from its entry, at the points shown.

**Keywords:** As in the core rules, a unit has the keywords of all its models, including its Sergeant and Auxiliary models: a Sangheili Warrior Cadre with an Unggoy Auxiliary is also an UNGGOY unit. Each species' own datasheets also have a unit keyword that its Sergeant and Auxiliary models do not: GRUNT (Unggoy), ELITE (Sangheili), BRUTE (Jiralhanae), JACKAL (Yig-Yar and T'vaoan), DRONE (Yanme'e), HUNTER (Mgalekgolo) and ENGINEER (Huragok). Rules for those keywords affect only that species' units; rules for UNGGOY, SANGHEILI and the other species keywords affect any unit with such a model in it.

**Toughness:** A unit's T characteristic is the one that the most models in it have. If two or more are tied, use the lowest.

**Issuing Caste Doctrines:** Once per battle round, at the start of any phase, each Sergeant model from your army can issue one Caste Doctrine from its entry to its unit. Unless otherwise stated, a unit can be affected by only one Caste Doctrine at a time.

## Shielding Trait

A model with an SP characteristic has Shields and starts the battle with that many Shield Points (SP).

**Shield Save:** Each time a model with 1 or more SP fails a save roll, determine the attack's Damage, then roll one D6.

■ 3+: the shield absorbs the attack. Subtract its Damage from the model's SP instead of its wounds; any excess is inflicted on its wounds.
■ 1–2: the shield is bypassed. All of the attack's Damage is inflicted on the model's wounds and its SP are not reduced.

**Regenerating Shields:** At the start of your Command phase, each unit with Shields regains D3 lost SP (D3+1 for VEHICLE and MOUNTED units, unless its datasheet says otherwise), split between its models as you choose. No model can exceed its starting SP.

**Damage That Ignores Shields:** Damage from [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] and psychic attacks ignores SP.

## Chosen of the Hierarchs

At the start of each battle round, if your WARLORD has this ability, gain 1CP.

## Caste Doctrines

**Sangheili Doctrines**

**Focused Assault:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to hit rolls for attacks made by models in this unit.

**Sangheili Ambush:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, select one of the following effects until the end of the phase: weapons equipped by models in this unit have [SUSTAINED HITS 1]; weapons equipped by models in this unit have [LETHAL HITS]; or unmodified hit rolls of 5+ made by models in this unit are critical hits.

**Tactical Withdrawal:** At the start of your Shooting or Charge phase, until the end of the phase, this unit is eligible to shoot or declare a charge, respectively, in a turn in which it made a fall-back move.

**Sangheili Special Operations Doctrines**

**Silent Approach:** At the start of your Movement phase, until the end of the turn, this unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it made an advance move, and enemy units cannot use Fire Overwatch against it.

**Decapitation Strike:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, attacks made by models in this unit have [PRECISION]. When such an attack targets a CHARACTER unit, re-roll a wound roll of 1.

**Fade Into Shadow:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, this unit has the Stealth ability.

**Jiralhanae Doctrines**

**Relentless Charge:** At the start of your Charge phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to charge rolls made for this unit.

**Savage Momentum:** At the start of the Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 2 to the S characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit if this unit made a charge move this turn.

**Unyielding Fury:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase or at the start of the Fight phase, until the end of the phase, subtract 1 from wound rolls for attacks that target this unit.

**Yig-Yar Doctrines**

**Pick Apart the Weak:** At the start of your Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to wound rolls for ranged attacks made by models in this unit that target a unit below starting strength or within range of an objective marker.

**Fire and Scuttle:** At the start of your Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, after this unit has shot it can make a normal move of up to 6". If it does, it cannot declare a charge this turn.

**Survival Above Honour:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, this unit has Stealth and improve the Shield Save of models in it by 1, to a maximum of 2+.

**Unggoy Doctrines**

**Desperate Survival:** At the start of any phase, until the end of the phase, models in this unit have Feel No Pain 4+.

**Vengeful Retaliation:** At the start of the Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, each time a model in this unit is destroyed, before removing it roll one D6. On a 4+, it can shoot if destroyed in the Shooting phase, or fight if destroyed in the Fight phase and it has not fought. Remove it after resolving those attacks.

**Courage Through Fear:** At the start of any phase, until the end of the phase, improve this unit's Ld characteristic by 2 and re-roll battle-shock rolls made for it.

**Yanme'e Doctrines**

**Swarm Convergence:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to hit rolls for attacks made by models in this unit while it contains 5 or more models.

**Predatory Descent:** At the start of your Charge or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to charge rolls made for this unit and melee weapons equipped by its models have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

**Fade Into Shadow:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, this unit has the Stealth ability.

## Supercombine

After this unit has made its hit rolls, for every three hits scored against the same target with [SUPERCOMBINE] weapons, that target suffers 1 mortal wound. Extra hits from [SUSTAINED HITS] count.

## Plasma

Each time an attack made with a Plasma weapon scores a critical hit, add X to that attack's D characteristic if the weapon has Plasma (DX), or improve that attack's AP characteristic by X if it has Plasma (APX). For example, a critical hit with a Plasma (AP1) weapon improves that attack's AP by 1. Extra hits from [SUSTAINED HITS] do not benefit.

## Conversion

Each time an attack made with this weapon targets a unit that is not within half range of that weapon, an unmodified hit roll of 4+ scores a critical hit.

## Gravity Slam

Once per phase, when a unit containing one or more models equipped with this weapon is selected to fight, before it makes its attacks, roll one D6 for each enemy unit within 3" of one or more of those models. On a 3+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds and must make a battle-shock roll.

## Siegebreaker

Each time an attack made with this weapon scores a critical hit, invulnerable saves cannot be used against that attack. Extra hits from [SUSTAINED HITS] do not benefit.

## Wargear Costs

Weapon exchanges are free, except: Fuel Rod, Skewer, two Needlers, Gravity Hammer and Twin-linked Energy Swords (+10 pts each) and Wrist Shield (+5 pts). A datasheet's own limits and costs take precedence.

## Transport Space

Each model takes up transport capacity by size: INFANTRY models 1 (MGALEKGOLO models 5), MOUNTED models 4 and VEHICLE models 8. A TRANSPORT whose capacity is given in Transport Space can carry any mix of eligible models up to that total, within the limits on its datasheet. AIRCRAFT, TITANIC and TRANSPORT VEHICLE models cannot be carried unless a datasheet says otherwise; MOUNTED transports such as the Revenant and Spectre can.
