# Covenant Army Rules (11e homebrew)

_Source: Codex: Covenant v5.5.0. Caste Hierarchy and Energy Shields are the revised versions; everything else is as printed._

## Caste Hierarchy

**Sergeants:** Some datasheets include a Sergeant model in their Unit Composition. A Sergeant model is part of its unit for all rules purposes, and its profile lists the Caste Doctrines it can issue.

**Auxiliaries:** Some datasheets let a unit add Auxiliary models at a fixed rate printed on that datasheet (for example, for every 5 Unggoy Warriors, 1 Sangheili Auxiliary or up to 2 Yig-Yar Auxiliaries). Each species has one Auxiliary entry: Sangheili, Jiralhanae, Yig-Yar, T'vaoan, Unggoy, Huragok and Yanme'e. T'vaoan Auxiliaries have the Yig-Yar keyword, so they can be taken wherever Yig-Yar Auxiliaries can. Each Auxiliary model costs the points shown on its entry and uses its own characteristics, weapons and abilities. It can also take one Specialist ability from its entry, paying the points shown.

**Keywords:** A unit's keywords are the keywords on its datasheet. Sergeant and Auxiliary models do not add their keywords to the unit; rules can still identify or affect an individual model by its own keywords.

**Toughness:** A unit's Toughness is the Toughness characteristic that the most models in it have. If two or more values are tied, use the lowest.

**Issuing Caste Doctrines:** At the start of any phase, each Sergeant model from your army can issue one Caste Doctrine to its own unit. Its entry lists the Doctrines it can issue. Each Sergeant can issue only one Caste Doctrine per battle round. Unless otherwise stated, a unit can be affected by only one Caste Doctrine at a time. A Doctrine lasts for the duration stated in its rules.

## Energy Shields

**Shield Pool:** A model with an SP characteristic is shielded. A unit containing shielded models has one Shield pool, which starts the battle equal to the combined SP of those models.

**Absorbing Damage:** Each time a model in a shielded unit fails a saving throw, if that unit's Shield pool has 1 or more SP, subtract the attack's Damage from the pool instead of inflicting it on the model. Any Damage in excess of the SP remaining is inflicted on the model as normal.

**Bypassing Shields:** Mortal wounds (including those inflicted by Devastating Wounds) and psychic wounds ignore Shields and are never subtracted from a Shield pool.

**Shield Regeneration:** At the start of your Command phase, each shielded unit from your army regains D3 lost SP (D3+1 for VEHICLE and MOUNTED units). If no enemy unit made an attack that targeted it during your opponent's last turn, it regains all of its lost SP instead. A datasheet that states a different amount uses that amount instead of D3 or D3+1. A Shield pool can never exceed the combined SP of the models remaining in its unit.

## Chosen of the Hierarchs

At the start of each battle round, if your Warlord has this ability, gain 1CP.

## Caste Doctrines

**Sangheili Doctrines**

**Focused Assault:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to Hit rolls for attacks made by models in this unit.

**Sangheili Ambush:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, select one of the following effects until the end of the phase: weapons equipped by models in this unit have Sustained Hits 1; weapons equipped by models in this unit have Lethal Hits; or unmodified Hit rolls of 5+ made by models in this unit are Critical Hits.

**Tactical Withdrawal:** At the start of your Shooting or Charge phase, until the end of the phase, this unit is eligible to shoot or declare a charge, respectively, in a turn in which it Fell Back.

**Sangheili Special Operations Doctrines**

**Silent Approach:** At the start of your Movement phase, until the end of the turn, this unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced, and enemy units cannot use Fire Overwatch against it.

**Decapitation Strike:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, attacks made by models in this unit have Precision. When such an attack targets a Character unit, re-roll a Wound roll of 1.

**Fade Into Shadow:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, this unit has the Stealth ability.

**Jiralhanae Doctrines**

**Relentless Charge:** At the start of your Charge phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to Charge rolls made for this unit.

**Savage Momentum:** At the start of the Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 2 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit if this unit made a Charge move this turn.

**Unyielding Fury:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase or at the start of the Fight phase, until the end of the phase, subtract 1 from Wound rolls for attacks that target this unit.

**Yig-Yar Doctrines**

**Pick Apart the Weak:** At the start of your Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to Wound rolls for ranged attacks made by models in this unit that target a unit Below Starting Strength or within range of an objective marker.

**Fire and Scuttle:** At the start of your Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, after this unit has shot it can make a Normal move of up to 6 inches. If it does, it cannot declare a charge this turn.

**Survival Above Honour:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, this unit has Stealth and improve the Shield Save of models in it by 1, to a maximum of 2+.

**Unggoy Doctrines**

**Desperate Survival:** At the start of any phase, until the end of the phase, models in this unit have Feel No Pain 4+.

**Vengeful Retaliation:** At the start of the Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, each time a model in this unit is destroyed, before removing it roll one D6. On a 4+, it can shoot if destroyed in the Shooting phase, or fight if destroyed in the Fight phase and it has not fought. Remove it after resolving those attacks.

**Courage Through Fear:** At the start of any phase, until the end of the phase, improve this unit's Leadership characteristic by 2 and re-roll Battle-shock and Leadership tests taken for it.

**Yanme'e Doctrines**

**Swarm Convergence:** At the start of your Shooting or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to Hit rolls for attacks made by models in this unit while it contains 5 or more models.

**Predatory Descent:** At the start of your Charge or Fight phase, until the end of the phase, add 1 to Charge rolls made for this unit and melee weapons equipped by its models have Sustained Hits 1.

**Fade Into Shadow:** At the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, until the end of the phase, this unit has the Stealth ability.

## Supercombine

After this unit has finished making Hit rolls, for every three hits scored against the same target by weapons with this ability, that target suffers 1 devastating wound. Additional hits generated by Sustained Hits count towards this total.

## Plasma

**Plasma (DX) / Plasma (APX):** Each time an attack made with this weapon scores a Critical Hit, add X to that attack's Damage if this weapon has Plasma (DX), or improve that attack's AP by X if it has Plasma (APX). Additional hits generated by Sustained Hits do not benefit. Automatically successful Hit rolls cannot trigger this ability.

## Siegebreaker

Each time an attack made with this weapon scores a Critical Hit, invulnerable saves cannot be made against that attack. Additional hits generated by Sustained Hits do not gain this effect.

## Wargear Costs

**Default Equipment (included):** Default equipment is included in the model or unit's listed points.

**Standard Exchanges (0pts):** Plasma Pistol; Mangler; Plasma Rifle; Storm Rifle; Covenant Carbine; Spiker; Needler; Plasma Repeater; Bolt Spiker; Brute Shot; Needle Rifle; Particle Beam Rifle; Stalker Rifle; Satchel Charge; Energy Sword; Brute Heavy Melee Weapon; Heavy Plasma Melee Weapon; Honor Guard Pike; ordinary close-combat weapons and plasma sabres; two Plasma Pistols; two Plasma Rifles; and standard mixed-weapon packages.

**Premium Exchanges (+10pts):** Fuel Rod; Skewer; two Needlers; Gravity Hammer; Twin-linked Energy Swords.

**Defensive Wargear (+5pts):** Wrist Shield.

A datasheet's exchange limits and eligibility still apply. Where a datasheet gives a more specific restriction, follow that datasheet.
