# Skaven Army Rules (11e homebrew)

_Exported from the codex source (rules_text.py). INVASION_BOX is now part of the Invasion of the Under-Empire detachment._

## The Vermintide

If your Army Faction is SKAVEN, you have a Vermin pool. At the start of the battle it contains 0 Vermin. Each time you gain Vermin, add it to your Vermin pool. Your Vermin pool can never contain more than 13 Vermin; any Vermin gained beyond that is lost. Vermin stays in the pool until spent.

### Gaining Vermin

**The Swarm Grows:** Start of your Command phase.

**Death Breeds Life:** Just after a friendly SKAVEN unit is destroyed, before resolving other rules triggered by its destruction.

**Panic-Panic!:** Just after a friendly SKAVEN unit fails a battle-shock roll.

**They Are Everywhere!:** Just after a friendly SKAVEN unit emerges from the Under-Empire.

**Disturb the Nest:** Just after a friendly Gnawhole is destroyed.

**Waste-Waste Them!:** The first time in each phase that one or more friendly models are Sacrificed.

**Feed the Swarm:** Just after an enemy unit is destroyed by an attack made by a friendly SKAVEN model.

**Take-Take Everything!:** Just after you gain control of an objective that was not controlled by either player, while one or more friendly SKAVEN units are within range of it.

### Paying Vermin

When a Vermin Ability has a cost of XD3 Vermin: roll that many D3, apply any modifiers, then spend that many Vermin. You cannot choose to pay less than the final cost.

### Thirteen for the Horned Rat

Once per turn, if you cannot pay the full Vermin cost of a Vermin Ability, you can instead Sacrifice exactly 13 eligible friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY models (excluding CHARACTER models). If you do, that ability's entire Vermin cost is paid and no Vermin is spent. If you can do neither, that ability cannot be used and no Vermin is spent.

### Limits

Each Vermin Ability can be used once per turn (once in your turn and once in your opponent's turn). Different Vermin Abilities can be used in the same turn. A timing window that passes cannot be saved for later.

### Rattletrap Surcharge

Each time a Vermin Ability targets or selects a RATTLETRAP unit, roll one additional D3 and add the result to its Vermin cost. Thirteen for the Horned Rat pays the entire modified cost.

### Vermin Abilities

#### More-More Bodies! (2D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** End of your Command phase.

- **TARGET:** One friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit that is below its Starting Strength.

- **EFFECT:** Roll 4D3+1. Return destroyed models to that unit whose combined Wounds characteristics do not exceed the result. You cannot return more than 13 wounds' worth of models, and the unit cannot be returned above its Starting Strength.

#### From Below-Below! (1D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** Just after making a Passage roll for a friendly unit, if the result was 2-3.

- **TARGET:** That unit.

- **EFFECT:** Resolve that Passage roll as a Found It result instead.

#### Menace Below (2D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

- **EFFECT:** Using Summoned Vermin, add a permanent minimum-sized CLANRATS unit to your army and set it up wholly within 6" of a friendly Gnawhole and more than 8" horizontally from all enemy models.

#### Knaw, Knaw, Knaw! (3D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

- **EFFECT:** Set up one friendly Gnawhole more than 8" horizontally from all enemy models. It follows all rules for Gnawholes.

#### They Just Keep Coming! (4D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** Just after a friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY, MOUNTED or RATTLETRAP unit is destroyed, after resolving Death Breeds Life.

- **TARGET:** That destroyed unit.

- **EFFECT:** Add an exact copy of the destroyed unit to your army at its Starting Strength, with all of its wounds remaining and the same wargear and Detachment upgrades, and place it in the Under-Empire. If the destroyed unit was an Attached unit, the copy is of its Bodyguard unit only; CHARACTER models are not added.

- **RESTRICTIONS:** This ability cannot target a unit containing only CHARACTER models, a Summoned Vermin unit or a unit created by this ability. If the destroyed unit had used a once-per-battle ability, the copy counts as having used it.

#### Take Them With Us-Us! (3D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** Just after a friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit that was engaged with one or more enemy units is destroyed.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit that was engaged with that destroyed unit.

- **EFFECT:** Roll one D6 for every 5 models that were in the destroyed unit at the start of the phase (maximum 6D6). For each 4+, the target suffers D3 mortal wounds.

#### Over Their Corpses! (3D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** Fight phase, just after a friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit is destroyed.

- **TARGET:** One other friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 6" of where that unit was destroyed.

- **EFFECT:** Your unit can make a surge move of up to 6". During this move it can move within Engagement Range of enemy models and can end the move engaged. If it has not fought this phase, it is eligible to fight this phase.

#### They're in the Walls! (1D3 Vermin)

- **WHEN:** Your opponent's Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a normal, advance or fall-back move within 13" of an unused friendly Gnawhole.

- **TARGET:** One friendly unit in the Under-Empire.

- **EFFECT:** Roll one D6. 1-3 - Lost-Lost!: That unit is destroyed somewhere in the tunnels; that Gnawhole is not used. When resolving Death Breeds Life for it, gain D6 Vermin instead of D3. 4+ - From the Walls!: That unit emerges wholly within 6" of that Gnawhole and more than 8" horizontally from all enemy models, and that Gnawhole is used.

## The Under-Empire

**intro:** If your Army Faction is SKAVEN, your army has access to the Under-Empire.

**into:** Each time a SKAVEN unit from your army is placed into Strategic Reserves, you can place it into the Under-Empire instead. Units in the Under-Empire are Strategic Reserves units and follow all rules for Strategic Reserves, except that they can only arrive on the battlefield by emerging. Units in the Under-Empire are not assigned to a specific Gnawhole.

**restricted:** MONSTER, TITANIC and VEHICLE units (excluding RATTLETRAP units) cannot be placed into, enter or emerge from the Under-Empire unless another rule explicitly allows it.

**emerging:** A unit emerges when it is set up on the battlefield from the Under-Empire, by Passage or by any other rule. Emerging is a type of ingress move with its own placement, in the same way as Deep Strike: set the unit up as described by Passage or by the rule that let it emerge, ignoring the normal ingress move placement rules (it can be set up within the enemy deployment zone).

**using:** Each Gnawhole can be used once per turn. A Gnawhole is used when a unit enters the Under-Empire through it or emerges from it. There is no other limit on how many units can enter or emerge each turn. If you have no unused friendly Gnawholes, units in the Under-Empire cannot emerge.

**first:** At the start of the Deploy Armies step, roll one D3, add 1 to the result and set up that many friendly Gnawholes, each more than 8" horizontally from your opponent's deployment zone.

**enter:** In your Movement phase, just after a friendly SKAVEN unit ends a normal, advance or fall-back move, or is selected to Remain Stationary, if every model in that unit is wholly within 6" of an unused friendly Gnawhole and more than 8" horizontally from every enemy model, it can enter the Under-Empire. Remove it from the battlefield and place it in the Under-Empire; that Gnawhole is used. A unit that emerged this turn cannot enter the Under-Empire this turn, and a unit that entered the Under-Empire this turn cannot emerge this turn.

**passage:** In the Move Units step of your Movement phase, when you select a unit in the Under-Empire to make an ingress move, select one unused friendly Gnawhole and make a Passage roll for that unit:

- 1 Lost Below: That unit is destroyed. The selected Gnawhole is not used. When resolving Death Breeds Life for that destruction, gain D6 Vermin instead of D3.

- 2-3 Wrong Turn: Randomly select one unused friendly Gnawhole (this can be the one you selected). That is the exit.

- 4+ Found It: The Gnawhole you selected is the exit.

**emerge_after:** The unit emerges: set it up wholly within 6" of the exit and more than 8" horizontally from every enemy model. The exit is used.

**illegal:** If the unit cannot be set up legally around its exit, set it up around another unused friendly Gnawhole where it can be; that Gnawhole is used instead. If no unused friendly Gnawhole allows a legal setup, that unit is destroyed. When resolving Death Breeds Life for that destruction, gain D6 Vermin instead of D3.

**spreading:** At the start of your Command phase in battle rounds 2-5, you can Sacrifice exactly 13 eligible friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY models (excluding CHARACTER models). If you do, create D3+1 friendly Gnawholes, each more than 8" horizontally from every enemy model.

**kh_token:** Gnawholes are 30mm tokens. They cannot move or make attacks. Enemy units can select a Gnawhole as the target of attacks as if it were an enemy unit; for every other rule it is not a unit (it is not a destroyed unit for mission purposes and does not block movement). Models can move over a Gnawhole but cannot end a move on top of it.

**kh_hidden:** A Gnawhole cannot be selected as the target of a ranged attack unless the attacking model is within 12" of it.

**kh_melee:** A Gnawhole can be selected as the target of melee attacks while one or more enemy models are within Engagement Range of it. An enemy unit within Engagement Range of a Gnawhole is eligible to fight, but can only target Gnawholes with its attacks. Units cannot declare a charge against a Gnawhole.

**kh_destroyed:** Resolve Disturb the Nest. Units in the Under-Empire are unaffected.

**kh_gnawdrill:** The Gnawdrill is not a Gnawhole token. Its datasheet specifies when it functions as a Gnawhole.

## Scurry Away!

**Cornered Rats:** Each time this unit fails a battle-shock roll, roll one D6. This unit suffers that many mortal wounds. If it is not destroyed, each remaining model can then move up to that many inches. Models can move within Engagement Range of enemy models; no model can end the move closer to any enemy model than it started; each model must end as far as possible from the closest enemy model; the unit must end in Unit Coherency.

**Live to Fight-Flee Another Day:** When this unit is selected to make a fall-back move, instead of selecting a fall-back mode, you can use this ability. Roll one D6. This unit suffers that many mortal wounds. If it is not destroyed, each remaining model can move up to that many inches; models can move within Engagement Range and each model must end the move no longer engaged with any unit it was engaged with. After the move, this unit becomes battle-shocked (this does not trigger Cornered Rats). Until the end of the turn, this unit is treated as not having made a fall-back move: it can make a normal move, shoot and declare a charge if otherwise eligible.

## [WARPSTONE]

Each time an attack is made with this weapon, if it scores a critical hit, add 1 to that attack's Strength characteristic; if it scores a critical wound, improve that attack's Armour Penetration characteristic by 1. TORRENT weapons make no Hit roll, so only the critical wound effect can apply to them. **Critical hits and critical wounds scored with this weapon cause Warpstone Backlash (on a 3+, the attacking unit suffers D3 mortal wounds).**

## Warpstone Backlash

Each time a unit shoots or fights, just after it has resolved its attacks, it must take one Warpstone Backlash test if one or more of its [WARPSTONE] attacks scored a critical hit, and one more Warpstone Backlash test if one or more of its [WARPSTONE] attacks scored a critical wound (at most two tests each time it shoots or fights). For each test, roll one D6: on a 3+, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Sacrifice

When a rule tells you to Sacrifice a model, select one model from your army on the battlefield that meets every condition of that rule and destroy it. No saving throw or other rule can prevent this. If that model cannot be destroyed, the Sacrifice fails and gives no benefit. Battle-shocked models can be Sacrificed. Models embarked within a TRANSPORT and models in Summoned Vermin units cannot be Sacrificed unless a rule states otherwise. Any other model can be Sacrificed unless the rule says otherwise; it does not have to be a SKAVENSLAVES or CLANRATS model.

## Summoned Vermin

When a rule tells you to add a unit using Summoned Vermin, add that unit to your army. Its Starting Strength is the number of models it is created with, and each model has only the default wargear from its datasheet (no Wargear Options or Enhancements). It is not a Reserves unit and cannot be placed into Strategic Reserves or the Under-Empire. Its models cannot be Sacrificed.

**Temporary units:** If the rule removes the unit at a set time, remove it from play at that time. It is not destroyed.

**Permanent units:** Otherwise, the unit remains for the rest of the battle.

You cannot gain Vermin from a Summoned Vermin unit being destroyed, being removed or failing a battle-shock roll. They Just Keep Coming! and More-More Bodies! cannot target a Summoned Vermin unit.

## Verminous Psychic Power

**Psyker Level:** Each PSYKER model has a Psyker Level on its datasheet. Each spell has a Psychic Level. Each battle round, a PSYKER can use spells whose combined Psychic Level does not exceed its Psyker Level; spend a spell's Psychic Level when you use it. At the start of each battle round, reset the Psychic Level each PSYKER has spent to 0. A rule that increases a Psyker Level raises that limit; it does not refund Psychic Level already spent.

**Using Spells:** A PSYKER can use a spell it knows when that spell's WHEN condition is met, if it is on the battlefield (unless a rule says otherwise) and its unit is not battle-shocked. Each PSYKER can use each spell once per phase, unless a rule says otherwise. Select the spell's targets, then make a psychic roll by rolling one D6. The spell resolves whatever the result. If the unmodified result is 1, after the spell resolves, that PSYKER's unit becomes battle-shocked and resolves Cornered Rats, even if it does not have Scurry Away!. This is not a failed battle-shock roll.

**Overcasting:** Once per turn, when a PSYKER uses a spell, before making the psychic roll, you can declare that it will Overcast. If you do, roll 2D6 instead of 1D6, reading each die separately, and resolve the spell's OVERCAST effect as well as its EFFECT. Overcasting does not increase the spell's Psychic Level. If either unmodified die is a 1, after the spell resolves, that PSYKER's unit suffers D6 mortal wounds, becomes battle-shocked and resolves Cornered Rats. Resolve this once per psychic roll, even if both dice are 1s.

**Re-rolls:** A rule that re-rolls a psychic roll re-rolls only the dice it states. No die can be re-rolled more than once. Check for unmodified 1s after all re-rolls.

**Psychic Weapons:** Weapons with [PSYCHIC] (for example Warp bolts, Pox blast and Shadow darts) are not spells. They use the normal attack rules, cost no Psychic Level, make no psychic roll and cannot be Overcast.

## Lore of Ruin

_Known by: Thanquol and Boneripper, Ikit Claw, Lord Skreech Verminking, Grey Seer, Verminlord Warbringer, Verminlord Warpseer. Warlock Engineers know Warp Lightning and Howling Warpgale only._

#### Warp Lightning (Psychic Level 1, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 24" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** That unit suffers D3+3 mortal wounds.

- **OVERCAST:** That unit suffers 2D3+3 mortal wounds instead.

#### Howling Warpgale (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** Your Movement phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 24" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the start of your next turn, subtract 2" from the Move characteristic of models in that unit (4" if it has FLY), and that unit cannot Advance.

- **OVERCAST:** Until the start of your next turn, also subtract 2 from Charge rolls made for that unit.

#### Death Frenzy (Psychic Level 2)

- **WHEN:** Start of the Fight phase.

- **TARGET:** One friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY or SKAVEN BEAST unit within 18" of this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit is destroyed by a melee attack, if it has not fought this phase, roll one D6. On a 4+, do not remove it yet: it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed.

- **OVERCAST:** Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in that unit.

#### Scorch (Psychic Level 1, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Roll one D6 for each model in that unit (maximum 13 dice). For each 4+, that unit suffers 1 mortal wound.

- **OVERCAST:** Each 3+ causes 1 mortal wound instead.

#### Crack's Call (Psychic Level 2, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One point on the battlefield exactly 18" from and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Draw a straight line from this PSYKER to that point. Roll one D6 for each enemy unit (excluding units with FLY) that has one or more models whose base or hull is crossed by that line. On a 3+, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds (D3+3 if it is a VEHICLE). Each unit can be affected only once each time this spell is used.

- **OVERCAST:** The point is exactly 24" away instead, and units are affected on a 2+ instead.

#### Flensing Ruin (Psychic Level 2, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** That unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. Until the end of the turn, worsen the Save characteristic of models in that unit by 1. This does not affect invulnerable saves.

- **OVERCAST:** Worsen it by 2 instead.

## Lore of Plague

_Known by: Thanquol and Boneripper, Lord Skrolk, Lord Skreech Verminking, Grey Seer, Plague Priest, Verminlord Corruptor._

#### Bless with Filth (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** Start of the Fight phase.

- **TARGET:** One friendly SKAVEN unit within 18" of this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in that unit have [LETHAL HITS].

- **OVERCAST:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a critical hit.

#### Pestilent Breath (Psychic Level 1, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 12" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** That unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. Until the start of your next turn, subtract 1 from Hit rolls made for models in that unit.

- **OVERCAST:** That unit suffers an additional D3 mortal wounds.

#### Vermintide (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

- **TARGET:** None.

- **EFFECT:** Using Summoned Vermin, add a temporary unit of 10 Clanrats to your army. Set it up wholly within 12" of this PSYKER and more than 8" horizontally from all enemy models. Remove it at the start of your next Command phase.

- **OVERCAST:** The unit has 20 Clanrats instead.

#### Wither (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the start of your next turn, subtract 1 from the Toughness characteristic of models in that unit (to a minimum of 1).

- **OVERCAST:** Subtract 2 instead (to a minimum of 1).

#### Plague (Psychic Level 2, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** That unit suffers D3+3 mortal wounds. Then roll one D6 for each other unit (friendly or enemy) within 3" of it. On a 4+, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. The plague does not spread further.

- **OVERCAST:** Roll for each other unit within 6" instead.

#### Pestilent Birth (Psychic Level 2)

- **WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

- **TARGET:** None.

- **EFFECT:** Using Summoned Vermin, add a temporary unit of 10 Plague Monks (including 1 Plague Deacon) to your army. Set it up wholly within 12" of this PSYKER and more than 8" horizontally from all enemy models. Remove it at the start of your next Command phase.

- **OVERCAST:** The unit has 20 Plague Monks (still including 1 Plague Deacon) instead.

## Lore of the Hidden Fang

_Known by: Lord Skreech Verminking, Verminlord Deceiver._

#### Skitterleap (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

- **TARGET:** One friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 12" of this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Remove that unit from the battlefield and set it up again anywhere on the battlefield, more than 8" horizontally from all enemy models. That unit counts as having made an ingress move.

- **OVERCAST:** Set it up more than 6" horizontally from all enemy models instead.

#### Warp Stars (Psychic Level 1, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy model within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER. It can be a CHARACTER model in an Attached unit.

- **EFFECT:** That model suffers D3 mortal wounds. Allocate them only to that model; excess damage is lost.

- **OVERCAST:** That model suffers 2D3 mortal wounds instead.

#### Armour of Darkness (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** Your Movement phase.

- **TARGET:** One friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 18" of this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the start of your next turn, improve the Save characteristic of models in that unit by 1. This cannot improve a Save better than 4+.

- **OVERCAST:** Improve it by 2 instead (still not better than 4+).

#### Veil of Shadows (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** Your Movement phase.

- **TARGET:** One friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 18" of this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the start of your next turn, that unit cannot be selected as the target of a ranged attack unless the attacking model is within 12" of it.

- **OVERCAST:** 6" instead of 12".

#### Brittle Bone (Psychic Level 1)

- **WHEN:** Start of the Fight phase.

- **TARGET:** One enemy unit within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in that unit (to a minimum of 1).

- **OVERCAST:** Until the end of the phase, also subtract 1 from the Attacks characteristic of those weapons (to a minimum of 1).

#### Black Whirlwind (Psychic Level 2, DAMAGE)

- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

- **TARGET:** One point on the battlefield within 18" of and visible to this PSYKER.

- **EFFECT:** Place a Black Whirlwind marker on that point; it remains until the start of your next Command phase. Each unit (friendly or enemy) within 3" of the marker suffers D3 mortal wounds. Until the marker is removed, each time a unit ends a normal, advance, fall-back or charge move within 3" of it, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. Each unit can suffer mortal wounds from this marker only once per turn.

- **OVERCAST:** Use 6" instead of 3".
