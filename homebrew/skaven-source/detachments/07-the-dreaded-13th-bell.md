# The Dreaded 13th Bell

## Daemonic Conduits

Each time a friendly SKAVEN non-DAEMON PSYKER uses a spell, before making the psychic roll, it can select one visible friendly SKAVEN DAEMON unit within 13" as its conduit. If it does:

- Measure that spell's range and visibility from either the PSYKER or the conduit.
- If that psychic roll has an unmodified 1, the PSYKER's unit does not become battle-shocked, does not resolve Cornered Rats and does not suffer mortal wounds. Instead, the conduit suffers D3 mortal wounds, or D6 mortal wounds if the spell was Overcast.

Each SKAVEN DAEMON unit can be selected as a conduit once per turn.

## The Thirteenth Toll

Each time a spell used through a Daemonic Conduit is Overcast, gain D3+1 Vermin, then each enemy unit within 13" of the conduit must take a battle-shock roll. Each enemy unit that fails that roll suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Grey Seer Enhancements

### GRIMOIRE OF THIRTEEN NAMES  [20 pts]

_Non-EPIC HERO GREY SEER model only._

The bearer knows one spell of your choice from the Lore of the Hidden Fang.

### BELL-RINGER OF THE FINAL HOUR  [20 pts]

_Non-EPIC HERO GREY SEER model only._

Once per battle round, when the bearer uses a spell through a Daemonic Conduit, it can Overcast that spell even if it has already Overcast this turn. After resolving that spell, the conduit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### THIRTEEN-EYED FAMILIAR  [15 pts]

_Non-EPIC HERO GREY SEER model only._

Once per battle round, after the bearer makes a psychic roll, you can re-roll one of its dice.

## Dreaded 13th Bell Stratagems

### RING-RING THE BELL!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just before a friendly SKAVEN PSYKER makes a psychic roll for a spell used through a Daemonic Conduit.

**TARGET:** That SKAVEN PSYKER.

**EFFECT:** Sacrifice one other friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY model within 6" of your PSYKER. Your PSYKER can Overcast that spell even if it has already Overcast this turn.

### DEVOUR THE BACKLASH  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a Daemonic Conduit from your army would suffer mortal wounds as a result of Daemonic Conduits.

**TARGET:** That Daemonic Conduit.

**EFFECT:** Reduce the number of mortal wounds suffered by D3+1, to a minimum of 0.

### VOICE OF THE HORNED RAT  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a spell used through a Daemonic Conduit has been resolved.

**TARGET:** That conduit and the SKAVEN PSYKER that used it.

**EFFECT:** Until your next Command phase, the conduit projects that PSYKER's Aura abilities as though it were that PSYKER. Duplicate instances of the same Aura do not stack.
