# Cauldron of Pestilence

## Brewing the Plague

At the start of your Command phase, you can brew one Plague. Select one Plague from the Plague Catalogue and pay its listed Vermin cost. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Plague is active and modifies the Pestilence auras of friendly PESTILENS units. You cannot brew the same Plague in consecutive battle rounds.

## Filth-Filth Carriers

CLANRATS units from your army gain the PESTILENS keyword and the following ability. **Plague Carrier:** This unit has a 3" Pestilence aura that carries the currently brewed Plague, but this unit does not gain the normal Pestilence ability. The brewed Plague also affects this unit as though it were an enemy unit within range of a friendly Pestilence aura.

## Infection

Each time an enemy unit is selected as the target of one or more attacks made by a friendly PESTILENS unit, after the attacking unit has finished making those attacks, if that enemy unit lost one or more wounds, roll one D6. On a 4+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds and becomes INFECTED until the start of your next Command phase. While a unit is INFECTED, the brewed Plague affects it as though it were within range of a friendly Pestilence aura. Each enemy unit can only be rolled for once per phase.

## The Plague Catalogue

_Brew one Plague at the start of your Command phase by paying its Vermin cost. "Pestilence range" means within range of a friendly Pestilence aura._

### THE WEEPING POX (2 VERMIN)

_Lesser Contagion_

While an enemy unit is within Pestilence range, worsen its Leadership characteristic by 1, and battle-shock rolls taken for that unit cannot be re-rolled.

### THE RED DEATH (3 VERMIN)

_Virulent Brew_

Each time a model in a PESTILENS unit makes a melee attack that targets an enemy unit within Pestilence range, an unmodified wound roll of 5+ scores a critical wound.

### THE CRAWLING AGUE (3 VERMIN)

_Virulent Brew_

Enemy units within Pestilence range subtract 2" from their Move characteristic, subtract 2 from advance rolls and subtract 2 from charge rolls. Multiple instances do not stack.

### THE BLACK ROT (4 VERMIN)

_Grand Plague_

Each time an enemy unit ends a normal, advance, fall-back or charge move while within Pestilence range, roll one D6. On a 4+, it suffers D3 mortal wounds. A unit can only suffer these mortal wounds once per phase.

### THE WITHERING SICKNESS (4 VERMIN)

_Grand Plague_

While an enemy unit is within Pestilence range, subtract 1 from the Strength characteristic of its melee weapons. If that enemy unit is below half-strength, also worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of its melee weapons by 1.

### NURGLITCH'S GIFT (7 VERMIN)

_Master's Concoction_

When this Plague is brewed, roll one D6 for each enemy unit within Pestilence range. On a 2+, that unit suffers D3+3 mortal wounds. Until the start of your next Command phase, while an enemy unit is within Pestilence range, subtract 1 from its Toughness characteristic, it cannot regain lost wounds, and destroyed models cannot be returned to that unit.

## Pestilens Enhancements

### ARCH-PLAGUEBREWER  [30 pts]

_PESTILENS CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, when you brew a Plague, the bearer can concoct a Grand Concoction. If it does, brew two different Plagues instead of one and pay both Vermin costs separately. Both remain active until the start of your next Command phase. This does not allow the same Plague to be brewed in consecutive battle rounds.

### CENSER OF THIRTEEN POXES  [20 pts]

_PESTILENS CHARACTER model only._

Add 3" to the range of Pestilence aura abilities of the bearer's unit, to a maximum of 8". Each time an enemy unit damaged by attacks made by the bearer's unit is tested for Infection, add 1 to that roll.

### TOME OF FOUL REVELATIONS  [20 pts]

_PESTILENS CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle round, the first time an enemy unit within 8" of the bearer becomes INFECTED, gain 1 Vermin. If that unit is a CHARACTER, MONSTER or VEHICLE, gain D3 Vermin instead.

### VIAL OF THE THIRTEENTH PLAGUE  [25 pts]

_PESTILENS CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, at the start of any phase, select one enemy unit within 12" of the bearer. That unit suffers D3 mortal wounds and becomes INFECTED; no Infection roll is required. If it was already INFECTED, it instead suffers D3+2 mortal wounds and remains INFECTED.

## Pestilens Stratagems

### INTO THE CAULDRON!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Command phase, when you brew a Plague.

**TARGET:** One friendly CLANRATS unit within 6" of a PESTILENS CHARACTER model from your army.

**EFFECT:** Sacrifice up to 3 models from your unit. Reduce the Vermin cost of that Plague by 1 for each model Sacrificed, to a minimum of 0. If two Plagues are being brewed, the reduction applies to only one of them.

### THE PLAGUE TAKES HOLD!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** At the start of the Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One INFECTED enemy unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, the target is treated as though it were within range of a friendly Pestilence aura for all purposes, including the normal Pestilence ability.

### FOAM-FROTH AT THE MOUTH!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** The Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One PESTILENS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, re-roll wound rolls of 1 for melee attacks made by models in your unit. If the target of those attacks is INFECTED, you can re-roll the wound roll instead.

### SPREAD-SPREAD!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after an enemy unit becomes INFECTED.

**TARGET:** One other enemy unit within 6" of that INFECTED unit.

**EFFECT:** Roll one D6. On a 3+, the target also becomes INFECTED.

**RESTRICTION:** An Infection caused by this Stratagem cannot trigger this Stratagem again.

### BLESSED-BLESSED FILTH!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit selects a PESTILENS unit from your army as the target of one or more attacks.

**TARGET:** That PESTILENS unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have Feel No Pain 5+. If the target is a CLANRATS unit with Filth-Filth Carriers, this Feel No Pain cannot prevent wounds or mortal wounds caused by the Plague it carries.

### LEAVE-LEAVE THE BODIES!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a PESTILENS INFANTRY unit from your army is destroyed.

**TARGET:** That destroyed unit.

**EFFECT:** Before removing its last model, place one Plague Corpse marker as close as possible to that model. Until the start of your next Command phase, enemy units within 3" of that marker are treated as though they were within Pestilence range for the purposes of the currently brewed Plague. A unit can only be affected by one Plague Corpse marker at a time.
