# Skryre Worship

## The Great Workshops

- **Workshop Projects:** when mustering your army, give Workshop Projects to up to 3 units (Incursion), 6 units (Strike Force) or 9 units (Onslaught). Each unit can have 1 Workshop Project, and each Workshop Project can be given to up to 3 units. A unit with a Workshop Project gains the SKRYRE keyword.
- STORMFIENDS units can be given WEAPON TEAM Workshop Projects.
- Workshop Projects are not Enhancements.

## Overstuffed Power Cells

Warplock carbines equipped by models in friendly CLANRATS units have [RAPID FIRE 2] instead of [RAPID FIRE 1].

## Volatile Genius

Each time a friendly SKRYRE unit suffers damage from its own shooting (a failed hazard roll, Warpstone Backlash or a Workshop Project), until the end of your next Shooting phase, add 1 to the Hit roll and the Wound roll for ranged attacks made by models in that unit.

## Workshop Projects

### SCRAP-WELDED ARMOUR

_BATTLELINE unit_

Each time a ranged attack is allocated to a model in this unit, improve that model's Save characteristic by 1 against that attack. Subtract 1" from this unit's Move characteristic.

### MORE-MORE BARRELS!

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Each time this unit is selected to shoot, you can re-roll any or all dice used to determine the Attacks characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit.

### WARPSTONE SUPERCHARGER

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Add 2 to the Strength characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit. Those weapons have [HAZARDOUS].

### MELTDOWN PROTOCOL

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Once per battle, immediately after this unit has shot, it can shoot again. After it has shot for the second time, roll one D6: on a 1-2, this unit is destroyed; on a 3-5, this unit suffers D6 mortal wounds; on a 6, nothing happens.

### VOLTAIC SHIELD AMPLIFIERS

_WARPCLAD unit_

Improve this unit's invulnerable save by 1, to a maximum of 4+. At the end of any phase in which this unit made one or more invulnerable saving throws, roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### REINFORCED-RAMSHACKLE HULL

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

Add 1 to this unit's Toughness characteristic and subtract 1" from its Move characteristic.

### WARP-LIGHTNING CAPACITORS

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

After this unit ends a normal, advance or charge move, select one enemy unit within 6" and roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 2-5, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 6, that enemy unit suffers 3 mortal wounds.

### DOOMROCKET

_Non-EPIC HERO WARLOCK ENGINEER model_

This model is equipped with a Doomrocket: Range 48"; Attacks 2D6+7; BS 4+; S 10; AP -2; D 2; [BLAST 1, HAZARDOUS, INDIRECT FIRE, ONE SHOT, WARPSTONE].

## Skryre Enhancements

### CHIEF-CHIEF ENGINEER  [30 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

Your army can give Workshop Projects to 2 more units, and one unit in your army can have 2 Workshop Projects instead of 1.

### SPARE RATS, SPARE PARTS  [25 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

In your Command phase, select one friendly SKRYRE VEHICLE, WEAPON TEAM or STORMFIENDS unit within 6" of the bearer. One model in that unit regains D3 lost wounds. Alternatively, select one friendly SKAVEN BATTLELINE unit within 3" of the bearer and Sacrifice D3 models from that unit. If you do, the repaired model regains 3 lost wounds instead.

### BRASS ORB  [25 pts]

_Non-EPIC HERO SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, in your Shooting phase, select one visible enemy MONSTER or VEHICLE unit within 12" and roll one D6: on a 1, the bearer suffers D6 mortal wounds; on a 2-3, the target suffers D3+3 mortal wounds; on a 4-5, the target suffers D3+6 mortal wounds and is battle-shocked; on a 6, the target suffers 13 mortal wounds and is battle-shocked. The Brass Orb is then expended.

### DOOMSPHERE  [30 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

After deployment, secretly select one objective in No Man's Land and record it. Once per battle, at the start of your Command phase during battle rounds 3-5, if the bearer is on the battlefield, you can detonate it. Units within 3" suffer 2D6+3 mortal wounds; units more than 3" and within 6" suffer D6+3 mortal wounds; units more than 6" and within 13" suffer D3 mortal wounds. Every surviving unit within 6" is then battle-shocked. Until the start of your next Command phase, all models within range of that objective have OC 0.

## Skryre Stratagems

### TEST IT ON THEM-THEM!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One SKRYRE unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase, and one friendly SKAVEN BATTLELINE unit within 3" of it.

**EFFECT:** Sacrifice D3 models from that BATTLELINE unit. Until the end of the phase, add 1 to Hit rolls and Wound rolls for ranged attacks made by models in your SKRYRE unit.

### THROW MORE WARPSTONE IN!  [1CP]

_Wargear Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a SKRYRE unit from your army is selected to shoot.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Strength characteristic and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic by 1 of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit; those weapons also have [HAZARDOUS]. If a weapon already has [HAZARDOUS], subtract 1 from hazard rolls taken for that weapon instead.

### NO-NO! THAT WAS SUPPOSED TO HAPPEN!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a SKRYRE unit from your army fails one or more hazard rolls.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE unit.

**EFFECT:** Re-roll any or all failed hazard rolls from that sequence. If one or more are still failed after those re-rolls, your unit becomes battle-shocked.

### EMERGENCY WARP-SHIELD  [1CP]

_Wargear Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit selects a SKRYRE VEHICLE unit from your army as the target of one or more attacks.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE VEHICLE unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save. At the end of the phase, your unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### SCURRY-SCURRY! IT'S GOING TO BLOW!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, just after a SKRYRE INFANTRY unit from your army has shot.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit can make a normal move of up to D6+2". It cannot embark within a TRANSPORT, end that move within Engagement Range, or declare a charge this turn.

### BLOW IT! BLOW IT NOW-NOW!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a SKRYRE VEHICLE model from your army is destroyed, before removing it from the battlefield.

**TARGET:** That destroyed model.

**EFFECT:** Do not roll for that model's Deadly Demise, Explodes, Catastrophic Warpcore or equivalent destruction ability. That ability automatically triggers. If the damage caused by that ability is determined using one or more dice, you can re-roll those dice.
