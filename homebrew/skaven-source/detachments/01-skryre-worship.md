## The Great Workshops

Skryre armies purchase Workshop Projects before battle by spending Workshop Tokens.

### WORKSHOP TOKENS

_When mustering your army, receive Workshop Tokens based on battle size._

| INCURSION | STRIKE FORCE | ONSLAUGHT |
| 1,000 points: 6 Tokens | 2,000 points: 12 Tokens | 3,000 points: 18 Tokens |

### PURCHASE PROJECTS

During Muster Armies, spend Workshop Tokens to purchase Workshop Projects from the following pages.

### ASSIGN PROJECTS

When a Workshop Project is assigned to a unit, record it on your Army Roster. That unit gains the SKRYRE keyword if it does not already have it.

### PROJECT LIMIT

A unit can have no more than 2 Workshop Projects. Projects not assigned to a unit, such as Hidden Workshop and Doomsphere, do not count toward a unit's Project limit.

### UNIQUE DESIGNS

Each named Workshop Project can be purchased only once per army.

### NOT ENHANCEMENTS

Workshop Projects are not Enhancements.

### UNUSED TOKENS

Unspent Workshop Tokens are lost when the battle begins.

## QUICK-QUICK CONTRAPTIONS  [1 TOKEN]

### JURY-RIGGED WARPLOCK OPTICS

_BATTLELINE unit with one or more ranged weapons_

Add 6" to the Range characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit.

### SCRAP-WELDED ARMOUR

_BATTLELINE unit_

Each time a ranged attack is allocated to a model in this unit, improve that model's Save characteristic by 1 against that attack. Subtract 1" from this unit's Move characteristic.

### SHOCK-COLLAR MOTIVATORS

_BATTLELINE unit_

Add 1" to this unit's Move characteristic. Each time this unit fails a battle-shock roll, you can re-roll that test. If that re-roll is also failed, this unit suffers D3 additional mortal wounds before resolving any other effects of that failed test.

### GYROSCOPIC TARGETING RIG

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit have [HEAVY]. Each time a model in this unit makes a ranged attack with a [HEAVY] weapon while this unit is benefiting from [HEAVY], re-roll a Hit roll of 1.

### WARP-FED DRIVE

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

Add 2" to this unit's Move characteristic. This unit is eligible to shoot in a turn in which it made an advance move.

### WARPSTONE STOCKPILE

_Select one non-EPIC HERO SKRYRE CHARACTER model_

Once per battle, when a friendly SKRYRE unit within 6" of that model would resolve Warpstone Backlash, that model can expend the stockpile. If it does, ignore that instance of Warpstone Backlash. This does not prevent damage from other rules.

## WARP-SPARK IMPROVEMENTS  [2 TOKENS]

### OVERSTUFFED POWER CELLS

_CLANRATS unit_

Change the [RAPID FIRE 1] ability of Warplock carbines equipped by models in this unit to [RAPID FIRE 2].

### VOLTAIC SHIELD AMPLIFIERS

_WARPCLAD unit_

Improve this unit's invulnerable save by 1, to a maximum of 4+. At the end of any phase in which this unit made one or more invulnerable saving throws, roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### OVERCLOCKED SERVOMOTORS

_WARPCLAD unit_

Add 2" to this unit's Move characteristic and add 1 to advance and charge rolls made for it. After this unit makes an advance or charge move, roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### MORE-MORE BARRELS!

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Each time this unit is selected to shoot, you can re-roll any or all dice used to determine the Attacks characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit.

### WARPSTONE SUPERCHARGER

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Add 2 to the Strength characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit. Those weapons have [HAZARDOUS].

## WARP-SPARK IMPROVEMENTS  [2 TOKENS]

### UNSTABLE POWER REGULATORS

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit by 1. After this unit has shot, roll one D6. On a 1-2, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### WARP-LIGHTNING CAPACITORS

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

After this unit ends a normal, advance or charge move, select one enemy unit within 6" and roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 2-5, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 6, that enemy unit suffers 3 mortal wounds.

### EMERGENCY WARP-SHIELD

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

Once per battle, when this unit is selected as the target of one or more attacks, models in this unit have a 4+ invulnerable save until the end of the phase. At the end of the phase, this unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### REINFORCED-RAMSHACKLE HULL

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

Add 1 to this unit's Toughness characteristic and subtract 1" from its Move characteristic.

### HIDDEN WORKSHOP

_Unassigned Project_

After deployment, place one Hidden Workshop marker wholly within your deployment zone. While a friendly SKRYRE unit is within 6" of that marker, you can re-roll hazard rolls taken for that unit. If an enemy unit ends any kind of move within 3" of that marker, remove it from the battlefield.

## GREAT-GREAT MACHINATIONS  [3 TOKENS]

### WARP-BLINK HARNESSES

_WARPCLAD unit_

Once per battle, at the end of your opponent's Shooting phase, if this unit is not engaged, you can remove it from the battlefield and place it in the Under-Empire, even if it is not within range of a Gnawhole. Then roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D3+1 mortal wounds. This does not require or use a Gnawhole.

### MORE-MORE POWER!

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Once per battle, when this unit is selected to shoot, until the end of the phase add 1 to the Damage characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit. After it has shot, this unit suffers D3+1 mortal wounds.

### MELTDOWN PROTOCOL

_WEAPON TEAM unit_

Once per battle, immediately after this unit has shot, it can shoot again. After it has shot for the second time, roll one D6: on a 1-2, this unit is destroyed; on a 3-5, this unit suffers D6 mortal wounds; on a 6, nothing happens.

### OVERLOADED WARP REACTOR

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

Once per battle, at the start of your Shooting phase, until the end of the phase improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit by 1; add 1 to fixed Attacks characteristics of those weapons; and each time a random Attacks characteristic is determined for one of those weapons, you can re-roll one of those dice. All ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit have [HAZARDOUS] until the end of the phase.

## GREAT-GREAT MACHINATIONS  [3 TOKENS]

### MORE-MORE ENGINES!

_SKAVEN VEHICLE unit_

Add 3" to this unit's Move characteristic and add 2 to advance rolls made for it. After this unit ends an advance move, roll one D6. On a 1, this unit suffers D6 mortal wounds.

### PORTABLE GNAWHOLE GENERATOR

_Select one SKRYRE unit_

Once per battle, at the end of your Movement phase, if that unit is not engaged, place one friendly Gnawhole wholly within 3" of that unit and more than 8" horizontally from every enemy model. Then roll one D6. On a 1, remove that Gnawhole and the unit carrying the generator suffers D6 mortal wounds.

### DOOMROCKET STOCKPILE

_Select one non-EPIC HERO WARLOCK CHARACTER model_

That model is equipped with a Doomrocket: Range 48"; Attacks D6+6; BS 4+; S 10; AP -2; D 2; [BLAST 1, HAZARDOUS, INDIRECT FIRE, ONE SHOT, WARPSTONE].

### BIG-BIG GUNS FOR BIG-BIG RATS!

_Select one STORMFIENDS unit_

That unit can purchase one Workshop Project from the Weapon Team Projects list as though it had the WEAPON TEAM keyword. It must be paid for normally, counts toward that unit's limit of 2 Workshop Projects, and does not otherwise grant the WEAPON TEAM keyword.

## RELICS OF RUIN  [4 TOKENS]

### BRASS ORB

_Select one non-EPIC HERO SKRYRE CHARACTER model_

Once per battle, in your Shooting phase, select one visible enemy MONSTER or VEHICLE unit within 12" and roll one D6: on a 1, the bearer suffers D6 mortal wounds; on a 2-3, the target suffers D3+3 mortal wounds; on a 4-5, the target suffers D3+6 mortal wounds and is battle-shocked; on a 6, the target suffers 13 mortal wounds and is battle-shocked. The Brass Orb is then expended.

## THE GRAND-GRAND DESIGN  [6 TOKENS]

### DOOMSPHERE

_Grand Project, Unique_

After deployment, secretly select one objective in No Man's Land and record it. Once per battle, at the start of your Command phase during battle rounds 3-5, you can detonate it. Units within 3" suffer 2D6+3 mortal wounds; units more than 3" and within 6" suffer D6+3 mortal wounds; units more than 6" and within 13" suffer D3 mortal wounds. Every surviving unit within 6" is then battle-shocked. Until the start of your next Command phase, all models within range of that objective have OC 0. The objective remains on the battlefield.

## Skryre Enhancements

### CHIEF-CHIEF ENGINEER  [30 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

When mustering your army, select one Workshop Project purchased for your army. Reduce its Workshop Token cost by 2, to a minimum of 1. A Grand Project cannot be selected.

### WARPSTONE HEART-CONDENSER  [20 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

Each time the bearer is selected to shoot, until the end of the phase add 2 to the Strength characteristic and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic by 1 of ranged weapons equipped by the bearer; those weapons also have [HAZARDOUS]. If one or more hazard rolls are failed for the bearer that phase, each failed roll inflicts one additional mortal wound.

### QUICK-QUICK ESCAPE HARNESS  [15 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, just after an enemy unit has finished making attacks that targeted the bearer's unit, if the bearer remains on the battlefield and is not engaged, the bearer's unit can make a normal move of up to 6". It cannot embark within a TRANSPORT, enter the Under-Empire, or end that move within Engagement Range.

### SPARE RATS, SPARE PARTS  [25 pts]

_SKRYRE CHARACTER model only._

In your Command phase, select one friendly SKRYRE VEHICLE, WEAPON TEAM or STORMFIENDS unit within 6" of the bearer. One model in that unit regains D3 lost wounds. Alternatively, select one friendly SKAVEN BATTLELINE unit within 3" of the bearer and Sacrifice D3 models from that unit. If you do, the repaired model regains 3 lost wounds instead.

### TESTED-TESTED! MOSTLY SAFE!  [10 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - non-CHARACTER SKRYRE unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Once per phase, you can re-roll one hazard roll taken for this unit. In addition, each time a Workshop Project causes this unit to suffer mortal wounds, reduce the total number of mortal wounds suffered by 1, to a minimum of 1.

## Skryre Stratagems

### TEST IT ON THEM-THEM!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One SKRYRE unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase, and one friendly SKAVEN BATTLELINE unit within 3" of it.

**EFFECT:** Sacrifice D3 models from that BATTLELINE unit. Until the end of the phase, add 1 to Hit rolls and Wound rolls for ranged attacks made by models in your SKRYRE unit.

### THROW MORE WARPSTONE IN!  [1CP]

_Wargear Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a SKRYRE unit from your army is selected to shoot.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Strength characteristic and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic by 1 of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit; those weapons also have [HAZARDOUS]. If a weapon already has [HAZARDOUS], subtract 1 from hazard rolls taken for that weapon instead.

### NO-NO! THAT WAS SUPPOSED TO HAPPEN!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a SKRYRE unit from your army fails one or more hazard rolls.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE unit.

**EFFECT:** Re-roll any or all failed hazard rolls from that sequence. If one or more are still failed after those re-rolls, your unit becomes battle-shocked.

### IT WORKED! DO IT AGAIN-AGAIN!  [2CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a friendly SKRYRE unit uses a once-per-battle Workshop Project.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE unit.

**EFFECT:** Roll one D6. On a 1-2, your unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. On a 3+, that Workshop Project can be used one additional time this battle.

**RESTRICTION:** Once per battle. A Grand Project cannot be selected.

### SCURRY-SCURRY! IT'S GOING TO BLOW!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, just after a SKRYRE INFANTRY unit from your army has shot.

**TARGET:** That SKRYRE INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit can make a normal move of up to D6+2". It cannot embark within a TRANSPORT, end that move within Engagement Range, or declare a charge this turn.

### BLOW IT! BLOW IT NOW-NOW!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a SKRYRE VEHICLE model from your army is destroyed, before removing it from the battlefield.

**TARGET:** That destroyed model.

**EFFECT:** Do not roll for that model's Deadly Demise, Explodes, Catastrophic Warpcore or equivalent destruction ability. That ability automatically triggers. If the damage caused by that ability is determined using one or more dice, you can re-roll those dice.
