# Plague Congregation

## Frenzied Devotion

- Friendly PLAGUE MONKS units have the BATTLELINE keyword, and add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in those units.
- In a turn in which a friendly PESTILENS INFANTRY unit made a charge move, melee weapons equipped by models in that unit have [LETHAL HITS].

## Enhancements

### BILIOUS PREACHER  [20 pts]

_PLAGUE PRIEST model only._

Add 3" to the range of the Pestilence aura ability of the bearer's unit.

### FRENZIED FAITHFUL  [10 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - PLAGUE MONKS unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Add 1 to charge rolls made for this unit.

## Plague Congregation Stratagems

### RABID ZEAL  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** The Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One PESTILENS INFANTRY unit from your army that made a charge move this turn and has not been selected to fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit. After your unit has fought, it suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### WALLOW IN FILTH  [1CP]

_Wargear Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit selects a PESTILENS INFANTRY unit from your army as the target of one or more attacks.

**TARGET:** That PESTILENS INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have Feel No Pain 5+.

### CHOKING CENSERS  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Start of the Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One PESTILENS unit from your army.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time an enemy model within 6" of your unit makes a melee attack, subtract 1 from the hit roll.
