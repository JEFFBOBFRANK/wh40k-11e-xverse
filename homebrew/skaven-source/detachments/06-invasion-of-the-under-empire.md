## Invasion from Below

### THE FIRST BREACH

Units from your army in the Under-Empire can emerge during the first battle round, even if the mission does not allow Strategic Reserves to arrive then. Each must still make its Passage roll and follow the normal rules for emerging.

### ENDLESS PASSAGES

Once per turn, you can re-roll one Passage roll.

### SURGING FROM BELOW

Each time a unit from your army emerges, until the end of the turn: add 1 to the Hit roll for ranged attacks made by models in that unit; melee weapons equipped by models in that unit have [LANCE]; and that unit counts as having Remained Stationary.

### MASTERS OF THE UNDER-EMPIRE

**Muster Below:** When you declare battle formations, any number of SKAVEN units from your army that could be placed into Strategic Reserves can be placed into the Under-Empire, ignoring the normal limits on the number of units and points that can start the battle in Strategic Reserves.

**No Reserve Deadline:** Units in the Under-Empire are not destroyed for failing to arrive by the end of the third battle round. They can remain below until the battle ends.

## Under-Empire Enhancements

### MAP OF THIRTEEN THOUSAND TUNNELS  [20 pts]

_SKAVEN CHARACTER model only._

At the end of your opponent's turn, if the bearer's unit is not engaged and is wholly within 6" of an unused friendly Gnawhole, you can place that unit into the Under-Empire. That Gnawhole is used.

### GNAWHOLE ARCHITECT  [25 pts]

_SKAVEN CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, at the end of your Movement phase, create one friendly Gnawhole wholly within 6" of the bearer and more than 8" horizontally from every enemy model. It cannot be set up within your opponent's deployment zone.

### FIRST CLAW ABOVE  [15 pts]

_SKAVEN CHARACTER model only._

In a turn in which the bearer's unit emerged, add 1 to charge rolls made for that unit.

### HOARDER OF SURFACE SPOILS  [15 pts]

_SKAVEN CHARACTER model only._

Each time the bearer's unit emerges within range of an objective, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit until your next Command phase. If you gain control of that objective that turn, gain 1 Vermin.

## Under-Empire Stratagems

### BACK BELOW-BELOW!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** End of your opponent's Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One unit from your army that can enter the Under-Empire, is wholly within 6" of an unused friendly Gnawhole and is not engaged.

**EFFECT:** Place your unit into the Under-Empire. That Gnawhole is used. Your unit cannot emerge during the same battle round.

### FLOOD THE SURFACE!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Movement phase, just after a unit from your army emerges.

**TARGET:** One other unit from your army in the Under-Empire.

**EFFECT:** Your unit can immediately emerge from the same Gnawhole, making its own Passage roll. This does not count toward that Gnawhole's once-per-turn limit.

### BURROWING CROSSFIRE  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One unit from your army that emerged this turn and has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have [IGNORES COVER].

### FROM EVERY TUNNEL!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Charge phase.

**TARGET:** One unit from your army that emerged this turn.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, add 2 to charge rolls made for your unit.

### COLLAPSE THE PASSAGE!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a friendly Gnawhole is destroyed.

**TARGET:** That Gnawhole.

**EFFECT:** Roll one D6 for each enemy unit within 6" of that Gnawhole. On a 4+, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### ARMOURED VERMIN-TIDE  [1CP]

_Wargear Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One RATTLETRAP unit from your army that emerged this turn and has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have [SUSTAINED HITS 1]. Warpstone Backlash still applies.
