# Shadowy Dealings

## Secret Schemes

### CHOOSE

After both players have deployed their armies, secretly select 4 different Schemes from the Hidden Ledger and record them.

### REVEAL

At the start of your Command phase, you can reveal one unrevealed Scheme. Once revealed, it remains active until it is completed or until the battle ends. Multiple Schemes can be active at the same time.

### COMPLETE

Each Scheme can only be completed once. You can complete a maximum of 4 Schemes during the battle. When a Scheme is completed, resolve its Payment immediately.


## Black Contracts

When mustering your army, select up to 3 CLANRATS units from your army. Those units gain the ESHIN keyword and the following ability. **Paid Informants:** This unit has the Scouts 6" and Stealth abilities.

## Death From the Shadows

Each time an ESHIN model from your army makes an attack that targets an enemy unit selected for one of your revealed Schemes, add 1 to the hit roll.

## The Hidden Ledger

### KILL-KILL THE HEAD

**Reveal**: Select one enemy CHARACTER model.  
**Complete**: That model is destroyed by an ESHIN unit.  
**Payment**: Gain D3+1 Vermin and 1CP.

### BURN-BURN THEIR TOYS

**Reveal**: Select one enemy VEHICLE or MONSTER unit.  
**Complete**: An ESHIN unit causes that unit to become below half-strength or destroys it.  
**Payment**: Gain D3+1 Vermin. Until the end of the battle, each time an ESHIN model makes an attack that targets an enemy VEHICLE or MONSTER unit, add 1 to the wound roll.

### STEAL-STEAL THE SECRETS

**Reveal**: Select one objective in No Man's Land or your opponent's deployment zone.  
**Complete**: At the end of your turn, you control that objective and one or more ESHIN INFANTRY units are within range of it.  
**Payment**: Gain D3+1 Vermin. One ESHIN unit within range of that objective can immediately enter the Under-Empire without requiring a Gnawhole.

### CUT-CUT THE SUPPLY

**Reveal**: Select one enemy non-CHARACTER unit.  
**Complete**: An ESHIN unit causes that enemy unit to lose one or more wounds during your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.  
**Payment**: Gain D3 Vermin. Until the start of your next Command phase, subtract 2" from that enemy unit's Move characteristic, it cannot make advance moves, and it cannot be removed from the battlefield and set up again by an ability.

### NOTHING-NOTHING WAS HERE

**Reveal**: Select one ESHIN unit.  
**Complete**: That unit ends your turn wholly within your opponent's deployment zone and is not engaged.  
**Payment**: That unit can immediately enter the Under-Empire without requiring a Gnawhole. Gain D3 Vermin.

### SILENCE THE WITNESSES

**Reveal**: Select one enemy INFANTRY unit.  
**Complete**: That unit is destroyed by one or more ESHIN units.  
**Payment**: Gain D3 Vermin. Select one ESHIN INFANTRY unit from your army that made one or more attacks against that enemy unit during the phase in which it was destroyed. That unit can immediately make a normal move of up to 6". Until the start of your next Command phase, that unit cannot be selected as the target of a ranged attack unless the attacking model is within 6".

### SABOTAGE-SABOTAGE!

**Reveal**: Select one objective in No Man's Land or your opponent's deployment zone.  
**Complete**: At the end of your turn, you control that objective, one or more ESHIN INFANTRY units are within range of it, and at least one such unit is not engaged.  
**Payment**: Gain D3 Vermin. That objective becomes Sabotaged until the start of your next Command phase. While Sabotaged, enemy models within range of it have an Objective Control characteristic of 0, and each time an enemy unit ends a normal, advance, fall-back or charge move within range of it, roll one D6: on a 4+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Eshin Enhancements

### MASTER OF A THOUSAND SCHEMES  [30 pts]

_ESHIN CHARACTER model only._

Select one additional Scheme at the start of the battle. Once per battle, when you reveal a Scheme, you can discard it and replace it with one different Scheme you did not originally select; reveal the replacement immediately. This does not increase the maximum number of Schemes you can complete.

### MASTER OF ESCAPE ROUTES  [15 pts]

_ESHIN CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, at the end of any phase, if the bearer's unit is not engaged, it can enter the Under-Empire without requiring a Gnawhole. When that unit next emerges, it does not use that Gnawhole's once-per-turn use.

### CLOAK OF SHADOWS  [20 pts]

_ESHIN CHARACTER model only._

Each time a ranged attack targets the bearer, subtract 1 from the wound roll. Once per battle, when the bearer is selected as the target of one or more attacks, until the end of the phase, enemy models cannot select the bearer as the target of an attack unless they are within 6" of it.

### WHISPER NETWORK  [15 pts]

_ESHIN CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle, if the bearer is on the battlefield, you can reveal one of your Schemes at the start of any phase instead of at the start of your Command phase.

## Eshin Stratagems

### EVERY RAT HAS A PRICE-PRICE!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Command phase.

**TARGET:** One SKAVEN unit from your army.

**EFFECT:** Until the start of your next Command phase, your unit gains the ESHIN keyword.

### MARKED FOR DEATH  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One ESHIN unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase, and one enemy unit that is the target of a currently revealed Scheme.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your ESHIN unit makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, re-roll a hit roll of 1 and a wound roll of 1. If that enemy unit contains a CHARACTER model, those attacks also have [PRECISION].

### SCURRY INTO THE SHADOWS  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an ESHIN INFANTRY unit from your army has finished making attacks.

**TARGET:** That ESHIN INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** If your unit is not engaged, it can make a normal move of up to 6". Alternatively, if it is wholly within 6" of an unused friendly Gnawhole, it can immediately enter the Under-Empire; that Gnawhole is used. A unit that makes the normal move cannot declare a charge this turn.

### CHANGE-CHANGE THE PLAN!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Command phase.

**TARGET:** One of your unrevealed Schemes.

**EFFECT:** Discard that Scheme and secretly select one different Scheme from the Hidden Ledger that you have not selected. Record it in place of the discarded Scheme.

**RESTRICTION:** Once per battle.

### SMOKE-SMOKE BOMBS!  [1CP]

_Wargear Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit selects an ESHIN INFANTRY unit from your army as the target of one or more ranged attacks.

**TARGET:** That ESHIN INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a ranged attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the hit roll, and models in your unit have the benefit of cover.

### POISONED BLADES  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** The Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One ESHIN unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have [LETHAL HITS]. Against an enemy unit selected for one of your revealed Schemes, those weapons have [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] instead.
