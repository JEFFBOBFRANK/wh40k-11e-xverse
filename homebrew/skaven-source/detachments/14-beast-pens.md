# Beast Pens

## Let Them Loose!

- Friendly **RAT OGORS** and **WOLF RATS** units have the **BATTLELINE** keyword, and add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in those units.
- In a turn in which a friendly **MOULDER BEAST** unit made a charge move, melee weapons equipped by models in that unit have **[LANCE]**.

## Enhancements

### GOAD-MASTER  [20 pts]

_MOULDER CHARACTER model only._

Once per turn, when a friendly MOULDER BEAST unit within 6" of the bearer declares a charge, you can re-roll that charge roll.

### OVERGROWN  [15 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - MOULDER BEAST unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Add 1 to the Toughness characteristic of models in this unit.

## Beast Pens Stratagems

### PROD-PROD THEM!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Charge phase, when a MOULDER BEAST unit from your army declares a charge.

**TARGET:** That MOULDER BEAST unit.

**EFFECT:** Add 2 to that charge roll. After your unit has finished its charge move, it suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### GORGE ON THE FALLEN  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a MOULDER BEAST unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

**TARGET:** That MOULDER BEAST unit.

**EFFECT:** One model in your unit regains D3 lost wounds.

### THRASHING DEATH-THROES  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** The Fight phase, just after an enemy unit selects a MOULDER BEAST unit from your army as the target of one or more melee attacks.

**TARGET:** That MOULDER BEAST unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed by a melee attack, roll one D6. On a 4+, do not remove it from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.
