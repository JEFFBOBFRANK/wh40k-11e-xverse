# Moulder Flesh Laboratory

## Flesh-Sculpting

- When mustering your army, give 1 Mutation to each of up to 3 units (Incursion), 6 units (Strike Force) or 9 units (Onslaught). Each must be a MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit.
- A unit with a Mutation can also take 1 Ruinous Defect. If it does, it takes a second, different Mutation.
- A unit cannot take a Mutation and the Ruinous Defect that changes the same characteristic. Bloated Mass and Withered Frame are for MONSTER units only. A Defect is taken only to gain a second Mutation.
- Mutations and Ruinous Defects are not Enhancements.

## Make-Make New Ones!

- Your They Just Keep Coming! Vermin Ability can also target a destroyed non-CHARACTER MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit. For a MONSTER unit, double the Vermin cost; if you use Thirteen for the Horned Rat instead, Sacrifice 26 models.
- The new unit keeps the Mutations and Ruinous Defects of the destroyed unit and takes 1 more Mutation. It can be placed in and emerge from the Under-Empire, even if it is a MONSTER.
- The new unit gains the MUTATED keyword and this ability. **Unstable Mutation:** This unit has Deadly Demise D6. Each time this ability is triggered, after resolving it, gain D3 Vermin.
- When They Just Keep Coming! creates a new CLANRATS unit, that unit can take 1 Mutation (not Bloated Mass) and gains the MOULDER and MUTATED keywords.

## Fresh Meat-Meat!

Each MOULDER CHARACTER model from your army has this ability: at the end of your Command phase, select one friendly MOULDER BEAST, MOULDER MONSTER or CLANRATS unit within 6" of this model, then select one other friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 3" of this model. You can Sacrifice up to 3 models from that SKAVEN INFANTRY unit. If the first selected unit is a CLANRATS unit, for each model Sacrificed, return one destroyed Clanrat model to that unit, not exceeding its starting strength. Otherwise, for each model Sacrificed, one model in the first selected unit regains 1 lost wound. Each unit can only be selected as the first unit for this ability once per phase.

## Pack-Driven

CLANRATS units from your army gain the MOULDER keyword and the following ability.

**Pack-Driven:** Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack, if this unit is within 6" of one or more friendly MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER units, add 1 to the hit roll.

## Feed the Beasts

Each time a friendly MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit is selected to fight, you can Sacrifice 2 models from one friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 6" of it. If you do, until the end of the phase, you can re-roll the hit roll for melee attacks made by models in that unit.

## Mutations

### THICKENED HIDE

Add 1 to the Toughness characteristic of models in this unit.

### CHITINOUS PLATES

Improve the Save characteristic of models in this unit by 1, to a maximum of 3+.

### TOO MANY LIMBS

Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit, excluding weapons with [EXTRA ATTACKS].

### OVERGROWN MUSCULATURE

Add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit.

### LENGTHENED LEGS

Add 2" to the Move characteristic of models in this unit and add 1 to charge rolls made for this unit.

### PREDATORY REFLEXES

Improve the Weapon Skill characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit by 1, to a maximum of 3+.

### BLOATED MASS

_MONSTER unit only_

Add 2 to the Wounds characteristic of models in this unit.

## Ruinous Defects

### SOFT FLESH

Subtract 1 from the Toughness characteristic of models in this unit.

### EXPOSED ORGANS

Worsen the Save characteristic of models in this unit by 1.

### MISSING LIMBS

Subtract 1 from the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit, to a minimum of 1, excluding weapons with [EXTRA ATTACKS].

### ATROPHIED MUSCULATURE

Subtract 1 from the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit.

### STUNTED LEGS

Subtract 2" from the Move characteristic of models in this unit.

### UNCONTROLLABLE

Subtract 1 from the Objective Control characteristic of models in this unit, to a minimum of 1.

### WITHERED FRAME

_MONSTER unit only_

Subtract 2 from the Wounds characteristic of models in this unit.

## Moulder Enhancements

### MASTER-BREEDER SUPREME  [30 pts]

_MOULDER CHARACTER model only._

Each time They Just Keep Coming! creates a new unit using Make-Make New Ones! while the bearer is on the battlefield, that unit takes 2 more Mutations instead of 1.

### NEEDLE OF THE GREAT STITCHER  [25 pts]

_MOULDER CHARACTER model only._

When the bearer uses Fresh Meat-Meat!: if the target is CLANRATS, each model Sacrificed returns 2 destroyed Clanrat models instead of 1; if the target is a MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER, each model Sacrificed restores 2 lost wounds instead of 1. Alternatively, if exactly 3 models are Sacrificed, you can return one destroyed non-CHARACTER model to a MOULDER BEAST unit with D3 wounds remaining. This alternative cannot return a MONSTER model.

### PRIME-SPECIMEN COLLECTOR  [15 pts]

_MOULDER CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle round, when an enemy CHARACTER, BEAST or MONSTER model is destroyed by the bearer's unit, gain D3 Vermin. If the destroyed model was a MONSTER, gain D3+1 Vermin instead.

### WARPSTONE PAIN-GOAD  [20 pts]

_MOULDER CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle round, at the start of your Charge phase, select one friendly MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit within 6" of the bearer. Until the end of the phase, add 2 to charge rolls made for that unit and it is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it made an advance move. After it completes a charge move, it suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Moulder Stratagems

### CRACK THE WHIP-WHIP!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Movement phase, when a MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit from your army is selected to make an advance move; or your Charge phase, when such a unit is selected to charge.

**TARGET:** That unit, if it is within 6" of a friendly MOULDER CHARACTER model.

**EFFECT:** Add 2 to that advance or charge roll and re-roll it. After your unit completes that move, it suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### FRESH PARTS!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after another friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY unit within 6" of a MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit from your army is destroyed.

**TARGET:** That MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the turn, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit, excluding weapons with [EXTRA ATTACKS], and one model in your unit regains D3 lost wounds.

### OPEN THE CAGES!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a MUTATED unit from your army emerges from the Under-Empire.

**TARGET:** That MUTATED unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the turn, add 1 to charge rolls made for your unit and melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have [LANCE]. If your unit is a MONSTER, it can also re-roll charge rolls this turn.

### STITCH-STITCH! STILL GOOD!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Command phase.

**TARGET:** One MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit from your army that is below its starting strength.

**EFFECT:** One model in your unit regains D3+2 lost wounds. If your unit has the MUTATED keyword, it regains D3+3 lost wounds instead.

### USE-USE ALL THE PARTS!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** When They Just Keep Coming! creates a new unit using Make-Make New Ones!.

**TARGET:** That unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit takes 1 more Mutation.

### NOT DEAD-DEAD YET!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a MOULDER BEAST or MOULDER MONSTER unit from your army is selected as the target of one or more melee attacks.

**TARGET:** That unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed by a melee attack before it has fought, roll one D6, adding 1 if your unit has the MUTATED keyword. On a 4+ (5+ for a MONSTER), do not remove that model. After the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, that destroyed model can fight and is then removed from the battlefield.
