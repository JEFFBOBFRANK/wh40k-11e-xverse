# Warlock Battery

## Overcharge the Guns

- Friendly WEAPON TEAM units have the BATTLELINE keyword, and add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in those units.
- Each time a friendly WEAPON TEAM unit is selected to shoot, it can suffer D3 mortal wounds. If it does, until the end of the phase, add 1 to the wound roll for ranged attacks made by models in that unit, and you can re-roll hit rolls of 1 for those attacks.

## Enhancements

### BATTERY OVERSEER  [20 pts]

_WARLOCK ENGINEER model only._

While the bearer is on the battlefield, each time a model in a friendly WEAPON TEAM unit within 6" of the bearer makes a ranged attack, re-roll a hit roll of 1.

### SPARE CAPACITORS  [10 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - WEAPON TEAM unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Each time this unit is selected to shoot, you can re-roll any or all dice used to determine the Attacks characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit.

## Warlock Battery Stratagems

### FIRE-FIRE TOGETHER!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, just after a WEAPON TEAM unit from your army has shot.

**TARGET:** One other WEAPON TEAM unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack that targets one of the enemy units hit by the first unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

### DRAG THE GUNS!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Movement phase, just after a WEAPON TEAM unit from your army makes an advance move.

**TARGET:** That WEAPON TEAM unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot in a turn in which it made an advance move.

### LEAN INTO IT!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One WEAPON TEAM unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack, you can ignore any or all modifiers to the hit roll and to the Ballistic Skill characteristic of that attack's weapon.
