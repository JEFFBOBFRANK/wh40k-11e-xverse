# Prophets of the Horned Rat

## The Horned Rat Whispers

- **Prophecy Dice:** at the start of each battle round, roll 3D6 (Incursion), 6D6 (Strike Force) or 9D6 (Onslaught) and set them aside. These are your Prophecy dice for that battle round. At the end of the battle round, discard all unused Prophecy dice.
- **Foretell:** each time one of the following rolls is made, after rolling but before resolving it, you can replace one of its dice with one unused Prophecy die. That Prophecy die is expended, and its value is treated as unmodified.
  - A psychic roll made for a friendly **GREY SEER** model.
  - A battle-shock roll, charge roll or Passage roll made for a friendly **SKAVEN** unit within 13" of a friendly **GREY SEER** model.
- After that roll has been resolved, that **GREY SEER** model's unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. For a roll made for another unit, the closest friendly **GREY SEER** model's unit suffers them.
- **Foreseen Glory:** if a Prophecy die showing a 6 replaces a die of a **GREY SEER** model's psychic roll, and that spell is not being Overcast, also resolve that spell's Overcast effect. This is not Overcasting.
- **Twist-Twist Fate!:** before using a Prophecy die, you can spend 1 Vermin. If you do, increase or decrease that Prophecy die by 1, to a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 6. Each Prophecy die can only be modified in this way once.

## Enhancements

### SEER OF THIRTEEN OMENS  [30 pts]

_GREY SEER model only._

At the start of each battle round, when you generate new Prophecy dice, if the bearer is on the battlefield, roll one additional D6, then discard one of the dice just rolled.

### WARPSTONE ASTROLABE  [20 pts]

_GREY SEER model only._

Once per battle round, when the bearer uses a Prophecy die, you can increase or decrease that die by 1, to a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 6, without spending Vermin. That Prophecy die cannot also be modified by Twist-Twist Fate!.

### BLOOD-PRICE FAMILIAR  [20 pts]

_GREY SEER model only._

Once per battle round, when the bearer's unit would suffer mortal wounds as a result of using one or more Prophecy dice, you can Sacrifice one other friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY model within 6" of the bearer. If you do, the bearer's unit does not suffer those mortal wounds.

## Prophets Stratagems

### CHANGE-CHANGE THE FUTURE!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Start of the battle round, just after generating your new Prophecy dice.

**TARGET:** Your newly generated Prophecy dice.

**EFFECT:** Re-roll any or all of those dice. You must use the new results.

### READ-READ DEEPER!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a roll made with two dice (such as an Overcast psychic roll or a charge roll) that is eligible for Foretell, before resolving it.

**TARGET:** The friendly GREY SEER model whose prophecy is being used.

**EFFECT:** When using Foretell for that roll, you can replace both dice instead of one, using two unused Prophecy dice. After resolving that roll, your GREY SEER model's unit suffers 2D3 mortal wounds instead of D3 as a result of using Prophecy dice.

### THE FUTURE IS FIXED!  [1CP]

_Epic Deed Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Just after a friendly GREY SEER model's prophecy is used with Foretell.

**TARGET:** That GREY SEER model.

**EFFECT:** Select one Prophecy die used for that roll. Do not expend that die; after resolving the roll, return it to your pool of unused Prophecy dice. The GREY SEER model's unit still suffers mortal wounds normally for using it.

**RESTRICTION:** Once per battle round.
