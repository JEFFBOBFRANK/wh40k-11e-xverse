## The Clawpack

### CLAWPACKS

During the Declare Battle Formations step, you can organize eligible units into Clawpacks. Each Clawpack consists of one VERMINUS CHARACTER unit and up to 3 CLANRATS and/or STORMVERMIN units. Each unit can belong to only one Clawpack. A CHARACTER does not need to Lead a unit in its Clawpack.

### UNDER COMMAND

While a CLANRATS or STORMVERMIN unit is within 8" of its Clawpack's CHARACTER unit, that unit is UNDER COMMAND.

### YES-YES, WARLORD!

At the start of your Command phase, each VERMINUS CHARACTER commanding a Clawpack can issue one Warlord Command to one UNDER COMMAND unit from its Clawpack. That Command lasts until the start of your next Command phase. A unit can normally only be affected by one Warlord Command at a time.

## Warlord Commands

### KILL-KILL THEM!

Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. If this unit contains 10 or more models, also re-roll a Wound roll of 1.

### MOVE-MOVE, COWARDS!

Add 2" to this unit's Move characteristic and add 2 to advance rolls made for it.

### HOLD-HOLD OR DIE!

Add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in this unit. While this unit contains 10 or more models, it can re-roll battle-shock rolls.

### STAB-STAB FROM BEHIND!

Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack that targets an enemy unit that is also engaged with another friendly SKAVEN unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

### SHIELDS UP-UP!

Each time a ranged attack is allocated to a model in this unit, that model has the Benefit of Cover. If this unit already has the Benefit of Cover, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1 instead.

## Clawpack Benefits

### RANKS UPON RANKS

While a CLANRATS unit is UNDER COMMAND and contains 10 or more models, improve its Leadership characteristic by 1 and add 1 to Hit rolls for melee attacks made by models in that unit. While it contains 20 or more models, also add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of its models.

### BLACK-FURRED ELITE

While a STORMVERMIN unit is UNDER COMMAND, each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack that targets an enemy unit that is engaged with one or more friendly CLANRATS units, add 1 to the Wound roll.

### NO RUN-RUN! FIGHT-FIGHT!

Once per battle round, when an UNDER COMMAND CLANRATS or STORMVERMIN unit fails a battle-shock roll, its Clawpack CHARACTER can enforce discipline if it is within 8". If it does, Sacrifice D3 models from that unit. That battle-shock roll is then treated as having been passed.

## Verminus Enhancements

### GREAT-GREAT WARLORD  [30 pts]

_VERMINUS CHARACTER model only._

The bearer can issue two Warlord Commands in your Command phase instead of one. Those Commands must be issued to different units from the bearer's Clawpack.

### WHIP OF DOMINATION  [15 pts]

_VERMINUS CHARACTER model only._

Increase the range at which units from the bearer's Clawpack are UNDER COMMAND from 8" to 13". Each time the bearer uses No Run-Run! Fight-Fight!, Sacrifice 1 model instead of D3.

### TROPHY-RACK OF THE CONQUEROR  [20 pts]

_VERMINUS CHARACTER model only._

Each time the bearer's unit destroys an enemy unit, until the start of your next Command phase, friendly UNDER COMMAND units from the bearer's Clawpack can re-roll battle-shock rolls and add 1 to charge rolls. If the destroyed enemy unit was a CHARACTER, MONSTER or VEHICLE, also gain 1 Vermin.

### BEST-BEST KILLER  [25 pts]

_VERMINUS CHARACTER model only._

Improve the Weapon Skill characteristic of melee weapons equipped by the bearer by 1, to a maximum of 2+. Each time the bearer makes a melee attack against a CHARACTER unit, re-roll a Wound roll of 1 and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

### RED-TOOTHED VETERANS  [10 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - CLANRATS or STORMVERMIN unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

While this unit is UNDER COMMAND, it can be affected by two different Warlord Commands simultaneously. Both Commands must have been issued by its own Clawpack CHARACTER.

## Verminus Stratagems

### GET IN FRONT-FRONT!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit selects a VERMINUS CHARACTER unit from your army as the target of one or more attacks.

**TARGET:** That VERMINUS CHARACTER unit and one friendly CLANRATS unit within 3" of it.

**EFFECT:** Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, each time an attack would be allocated to a model in your VERMINUS CHARACTER unit, if the selected CLANRATS unit is an eligible target for that attack, allocate that attack to the CLANRATS unit instead.

### CLOSE-CLOSE THE TRAP!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** The Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One UNDER COMMAND CLANRATS or STORMVERMIN unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a melee attack that targets an enemy unit that is engaged with two or more other friendly SKAVEN units, add 1 to the Wound roll and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

### HOLD-HOLD! MORE RATS COMING!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an UNDER COMMAND CLANRATS unit from your army loses one or more models.

**TARGET:** That CLANRATS unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have Feel No Pain 6+. In addition, when determining whether your unit contains 10 or 20 models for Ranks Upon Ranks or Warlord Commands, treat it as containing 5 additional models.

### PUSH-PUSH THEM FORWARD!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Movement phase, when an UNDER COMMAND CLANRATS or STORMVERMIN unit from your army makes an advance move; or your Charge phase, when such a unit declares a charge.

**TARGET:** That unit.

**EFFECT:** Sacrifice up to D3 models from your unit. For each model Sacrificed: if making an advance move, add 1" to its Move characteristic until the end of the phase; if charging, add 1 to its charge roll. If 3 models were Sacrificed, also re-roll that advance or charge roll.

### BLACK-FURS! KILL-KILL!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Charge phase, just after an enemy unit ends a charge move engaged with a friendly CLANRATS unit.

**TARGET:** One friendly STORMVERMIN unit from the same Clawpack that is within 6" of that CLANRATS unit and is not engaged.

**EFFECT:** Your STORMVERMIN unit can immediately declare a charge that targets only that enemy unit. If the charge is successful, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have [LANCE] until the end of the turn.

### NEW ORDERS! NEW ORDERS!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Any phase.

**TARGET:** One UNDER COMMAND CLANRATS or STORMVERMIN unit from your army that is currently affected by a Warlord Command.

**EFFECT:** Replace that Warlord Command with a different Warlord Command. The new Command lasts for the normal duration. If your unit has Red-Toothed Veterans, replace only one of its Commands.
