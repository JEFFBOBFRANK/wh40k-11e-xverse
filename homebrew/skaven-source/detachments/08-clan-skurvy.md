## Plunder and Profit

### KEEPING PLUNDER

Each SKURVY unit begins the battle with 0 Plunder. Record each unit's Plunder separately. When a unit is destroyed, its Plunder is lost.

### SPOILS OF BATTLE

Each time a SKURVY unit from your army destroys an enemy unit, it gains 1 Plunder.

### PLUNDERING OBJECTIVES

In your Movement phase, just after a SKURVY unit from your army ends a normal move, if it is not battle-shocked, it can Plunder one objective within range that has not been Plundered by your army this turn. If it does, that unit gains 3 Plunder and cannot declare a charge this turn.

### SPENDING PLUNDER

At the end of your Command phase, each SKURVY unit from your army that is on the battlefield and is not battle-shocked can purchase one option from the Plunder Ledger. Spend the listed amount of that unit's Plunder. Unless otherwise stated, that option lasts until the start of your next Command phase. A unit can have no more than two different Plunder options active at the same time.

### BLACK-FLAG CLANRATS

CLANRATS units from your army gain the SKURVY keyword and the following ability.

**Claim-Claim the Prize!:** At the end of your Movement phase, if this unit is within range of an objective you control, that objective remains under your control until your opponent's Level of Control over it is greater than yours at the end of a phase.

### BLACK-FLAG MERCENARIES

When mustering your army, you can include units from Index: Black-Flag. Their combined points value cannot exceed half of your army's points limit. Each unit included this way gains the SKURVY keyword.

## The Plunder Ledger

### QUICK-QUICK LOOT  [1 PLUNDER]

**Stolen Ammo:** Ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit have [IGNORES COVER].

**Extra-Extra Dakka!:** Ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

**Grav-Hooks and Boosters:** Add 2" to this unit's Move characteristic. This unit is eligible to shoot in a turn in which it made an advance move.

### PRIZED SPOILS  [2 PLUNDER]

**Shiny-Shiny Shields!:** Models in this unit have a 5+ invulnerable save. If they already have an invulnerable save, improve it by 1, to a maximum of 4+.

**Targeting Gubbins:** Improve the Ballistic Skill characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit by 1, to a maximum of 2+.

**Stolen Combat Drugs:** Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit, excluding weapons with [EXTRA ATTACKS]. Add 1 to advance and charge rolls made for this unit.

### THE GOOD-GOOD STUFF  [3 PLUNDER]

**Warp-Charged Munitions:** Improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit by 1. Those weapons have [WARPSTONE].

**The Really Good Stuff:** Select two different 1-Plunder options. This unit gains both.

### THE CAPTAIN'S SHARE  [4 PLUNDER]

**Aeldari Grav-Drives:** Add 3" to this unit's Move characteristic. This unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it made an advance or fall-back move.

## Skurvy Enhancements

_Officers & Specialists_

### CAPTAIN  [20 pts]

_SKAVEN CHARACTER model only._

At the end of your Command phase, select one other friendly SKURVY unit within 6" of the bearer. You can transfer up to 2 Plunder from the bearer's unit to that unit, or from that unit to the bearer's unit.

### MASTER-AT-ARMS  [15 pts]

_SKURVY CHARACTER model only._

Add the following option to the Plunder Ledger for the bearer's unit: 3 Plunder - Bloody Boarding: Until the start of your next Command phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit by 1 and those weapons have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

### MASTER GUNNER  [15 pts]

_SKURVY CHARACTER model only._

Add the following option to the Plunder Ledger for the bearer's unit: 2 Plunder - Loaded for Bear: Until the start of your next Command phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit have [LETHAL HITS].

### MASTER HELMSMAN  [20 pts]

_SKURVY CHARACTER model only._

Add the following option to the Plunder Ledger for the bearer's unit: 3 Plunder - Hit-and-Run Rigging: Until the start of your next Command phase, after this unit has shot, if it is not engaged, it can make a normal move of up to 6". If it does, it cannot declare a charge this turn.

_Upgrade Enhancements_

### BLACK-FUR BOARDERS UPGRADE  [10 pts]

_STORMVERMIN unit only._

This unit gains the SKURVY keyword. Add 1 to charge rolls made for this unit. Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack that targets an enemy unit within range of an objective, re-roll a Hit roll of 1.

### PRIZE GUNNERY CREWS UPGRADE  [15 pts]

_WEAPON TEAM unit only._

This unit gains the SKURVY keyword. While this unit has one or more Plunder Ledger options active, each time a model in this unit makes a ranged attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1.

### BLACK-FLAG RIGGING UPGRADE  [15 pts]

_SKAVEN VEHICLE or SKAVEN TRANSPORT unit only._

This unit gains the SKURVY keyword. Add 2" to this unit's Move characteristic. In your Movement phase, just after this unit ends an advance move, it can Plunder an objective within range as if it had ended a normal move. If it does, it cannot shoot or declare a charge this turn.

## Clan Skurvy Stratagems

### BOARDING PARTY  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One SKURVY unit from your army that made a charge move this turn and has not been selected to fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have [LANCE].

### CUT AND RUN  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** End of the Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One SKURVY unit from your army that is engaged.

**EFFECT:** Your unit can make a fall-back move of up to 6".

### BRACE FOR RETURN FIRE  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit selects its targets.

**TARGET:** One SKURVY unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks.

**EFFECT:** Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

### POINT-BLANK VOLLEY  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One SKURVY unit from your army, excluding MONSTER and VEHICLE units, that is engaged and has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit, excluding [BLAST] weapons, have [CLOSE-QUARTERS].

### LOOT THE DEAD  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** End of any phase.

**TARGET:** One SKURVY unit from your army that destroyed one or more enemy units this phase.

**EFFECT:** Your unit gains 1 Plunder.

### OVER THE GUNWALES!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Movement phase, just after a SKURVY TRANSPORT unit from your army ends an advance move.

**TARGET:** That SKURVY TRANSPORT unit.

**EFFECT:** One unit embarked within your TRANSPORT can make a Shock Disembark move.
