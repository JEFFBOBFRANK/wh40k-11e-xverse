# Night Stalkers

## Unseen, Unheard

- Friendly NIGHT RUNNERS units have the BATTLELINE keyword, and add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in those units.
- Friendly ESHIN INFANTRY units have the Stealth ability, and are eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which they made a fall-back move.

## Enhancements

### SHADOW BLADE  [20 pts]

_DEATHMASTER model only._

Melee weapons equipped by the bearer have [PRECISION], and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of those weapons by 1.

### WEEPING BLADES  [10 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - ESHIN INFANTRY unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Melee weapons equipped by models in this unit have [LETHAL HITS].

## Night Stalkers Stratagems

### VANISH-VANISH!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, just after an ESHIN INFANTRY unit from your army has shot.

**TARGET:** That ESHIN INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** If your unit is not engaged, it can make a normal move of up to 6". It cannot declare a charge this turn.

### RAIN OF STARS  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One ESHIN unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit.

### STRIKE FROM DARKNESS  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One ESHIN unit from your army that arrived from Reserves, emerged from the Under-Empire or made a Scout move this battle round, and has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the hit roll and the wound roll for attacks made by models in your unit.
