# Engines of Ruin

## Drive the Engines Hard

- Add 2 to advance rolls made for friendly SKAVEN VEHICLE units. Friendly SKAVEN VEHICLE units are eligible to shoot in a turn in which they made an advance move.
- **Engine Strain:** at the end of your Shooting phase, each friendly SKAVEN VEHICLE unit that made an advance move this turn and was selected to shoot this phase must take a hazard roll.

## Enhancements

### MASTER OF THE REDLINE  [25 pts]

_SKAVEN CHARACTER model only._

In your Command phase, select one friendly SKAVEN VEHICLE unit within 6" of the bearer. Until the start of your next Command phase, each time that unit takes its Engine Strain hazard roll, you can re-roll it.

### WARP-FED GEARBOX  [20 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - SKAVEN VEHICLE unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Add 2" to this unit's Move characteristic. Each time an advance roll is made for this unit, you can re-roll it.

### JURY-RIGGED STABILIZERS  [15 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - SKAVEN VEHICLE unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

Each time a model in this unit makes a ranged attack in a turn in which this unit made an advance move, you can ignore any or all modifiers to that attack's hit roll and to the Ballistic Skill characteristic of that attack's weapon.

## Engines Stratagems

### MORE-MORE POWER TO THE ENGINES!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Movement phase, when a SKAVEN VEHICLE unit from your army is selected to make an advance move.

**TARGET:** That SKAVEN VEHICLE unit.

**EFFECT:** Do not make an advance roll for your unit. Instead, until the end of the phase, add 8" to its Move characteristic. If your unit is selected to shoot this turn, add 1 to its Engine Strain hazard roll this phase.

### HOLD IT STEADY-STEADY!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase.

**TARGET:** One SKAVEN VEHICLE unit from your army that made an advance move this turn and has not been selected to shoot this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have [IGNORES COVER] and each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack, re-roll a hit roll of 1. After your unit has finished making its attacks, add 1 to its Engine Strain hazard roll this phase.

### NO BRAKES! NO BRAKES!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, just after a SKAVEN VEHICLE unit from your army that made an advance move this turn has finished making its attacks.

**TARGET:** That SKAVEN VEHICLE unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit can make a normal move of up to 6". It cannot end that move within engagement range. At the end of the phase, your unit automatically fails its Engine Strain hazard roll.
