# Endless Swarm

## There Are Always More

- Melee weapons equipped by models in friendly CLANRATS units have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].
- While a friendly CLANRATS unit contains 20 or more models, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit.

## Enhancements

### WHIP-MASTER  [20 pts]

_VERMINUS CHARACTER model only._

Once per battle round, when a friendly CLANRATS unit within 6" of the bearer takes a battle-shock roll, you can re-roll that test.

### BLACK-TOOTHED MOB  [10 pts]

_UNIT ENHANCEMENT - CLANRATS unit only; up to three units can have this Enhancement._

You can re-roll charge rolls made for this unit.

## Endless Swarm Stratagems

### SWARM THEM!  [1CP]

_Battle Tactic Stratagem_

**WHEN:** The Fight phase.

**TARGET:** One CLANRATS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a melee attack that targets an enemy unit engaged with two or more friendly SKAVEN units, add 1 to the hit roll.

### TOO MANY TO COUNT  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit selects a CLANRATS unit from your army that contains 20 or more models as the target of one or more attacks.

**TARGET:** That CLANRATS unit.

**EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have Feel No Pain 6+.

### FILL THE GAPS!  [1CP]

_Strategic Ploy Stratagem_

**WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

**TARGET:** One CLANRATS unit from your army and one friendly SKAVENSLAVES unit within 6" of it.

**EFFECT:** Sacrifice up to D3 models from that SKAVENSLAVES unit. For each model Sacrificed, return one destroyed Clanrat model to your CLANRATS unit, not exceeding its starting strength.
